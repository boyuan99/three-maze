Brute-force resolution: side 0.5-degree x (top - 0.3) / 240 in height, caps 1 degree x r / 60: it over-estimates the true minimum by at most ~ r (1 - cos 0.25 deg) + slope x half-step, ~1e-3 on tilted faces;
so differences of order 1e-3 mean agreement within the brute force resolution, and a method BELOW the brute force by more than that would indicate brute-force undersampling, not method error.
