# ADR-0001: Move the player per device sample with a hover kinematic character controller on compiler-partitioned colliders

- Status: Accepted (2026-10-08)
- Date: 2026-10-07
- Deciders: project owner
- Related: [0002-task-engine-location.md](0002-task-engine-location.md), [0003-render-worker-priority.md](0003-render-worker-priority.md)
- Roadmap: phase 0, "write 3 architecture decision records (ADRs)"; unblocks phase 1 (motion core, `compileScene`, maze migrator) before v1.0-rc, the first build allowed to collect paper data.

## Context

In v0.3 (HEAD a81fd77) the player is a dynamic Rapier ball. The backend turns each sensor line into a velocity assuming 50 ms per sample (`experiments/hallway04_experiment.py:81`, `:532-541`); the renderer holds the newest velocity (`src/scenes/serial/PythonCustomScene.vue:148-155`, `:300`) and steps Rapier 1/60 s per `requestAnimationFrame` (rAF) callback (`:222`). So **g = (rAF rate / 60) · (T_dev / 50 ms)** for true sample period T_dev (2.40 at 144 Hz); step 0 (`src/utils/frameClock.js:4-6`; `electron/main.js:135`, `:178`) only detects and reduces this. Heading is integrated twice, the renderer's copy only from surviving messages (`experiments/hallway04_experiment.py:287-289`, likewise hallway01/02; `PythonCustomScene.vue:158-159`). Eye height is 3.7 units (ball centre + 1.6, `:235`, plus camera 1.6, `:574`; comments suggest 1.6 or 2.1) and sags with speed (gravity −500, `:623`; friction, `:497`). Without continuous collision detection (CCD) the ball tunnels through 1-unit walls above about 65 units/s, within the speed cap (`experiments/hallway04_experiment.py:80`).

Options B and C below need opposite collider partitioning, so the phase-1 compiler, continuous integration (CI) suite and simulator wait on this choice. At a81fd77 Rapier was pinned only by `package-lock.json` (0.14.0) and `package.json:22` allowed any 0.14.x; phase 0 pins it exactly (R8).

**Terms.** *Device sample*: one motion-sensor record (sequence number seq, device timestamp t_dev, body-frame displacement). *Motion core*: consumes samples in seq order, each once; owns heading, mover and *zones* (sensor volumes emitting enter and exit events); runs in the *motion worker* (a Web Worker in the scene window, never the Vue main thread) and headless in Node. *Rig runtime*: the Python process serving devices and recording data. *Epoch*: a teleport and reset counter, replacing v0.3's coordinate-based stale-pose filter (`backend/src/main.py:625-638`, armed at `:644-645`).

## Decision drivers

1. Exact gain and heading on any display; exact replay.
2. Never deeper in a wall than the 0.02 *skin* (the gap the mover keeps), never through it, up to 141 units/s and 50 units per sample.
3. Declared eye height; falls (Hallway02 and Hallway_fog have open edges, `experiments/hallway02_experiment.py:447-475`), steps and inclined objects, which the owner requires for some scenes (2026-10-07).
4. One tested module for rig, simulation, CI and agent tools, far cheaper than a sample period at 1,000 Hz.

## Considered options

**A. v0.3 ball**, optionally patched by the one-day "H0" hotfix. Pro: smallest change (H0 simulates gain 0.998–1.000). Con: still timed by frames, so a bridge only.

**B. SlideMover** (motion-core study): a 0.5-radius ball swept with `castShape`, held at the skin by exact `contactShape` distances, guarded after every sub-step of at most 0.25 units. Pros: never overlapped; no wobble on merged walls. Cons: trapped at coplanar seams between separate colliders when pushed 80–89° into the wall (angles from the wall surface; 90° is head-on). Its design merges collinear walls, removing some seams (Hallway01/04's z = ±70), not all; the random soak never triggered the trap. The tested mover ignores floors; the design adds a floor probe and falls (not adjudicated) but no steps.

**C. Hover KCC, per device sample** (the adjudication's resolution: the physics-binding study's mover in the motion-core architecture): Rapier's kinematic character controller (KCC) slides an upright cylinder floating one step height above the floor; a ground ray sets the height (R3, R4). Rapier's stock usage, a capsule on the floor with snap-to-ground, varied eye height by 0.013–0.29 units even with nudge 0.01 and floor tiles ≤ 40. Pros: seams harmless; exact eye height; falls, steps, shallow ramps; no overlap with colliders capped at 40 units. Cons: mandatory cap; sideways wobble; version-sensitive; unsafe with the default `normalNudgeFactor`, 1e-4 (105 of 24,000 soak calls at 20 samples/s overlapped); creeps into inclined faces unless a wall rule is added (the Decision adds one).

**D. Hover KCC on a fixed 120 Hz tick** (the physics-binding design as proposed), over a packet timeline delayed by L = 0.06 s. Pro: identical trajectories at 60–165 Hz rendering. Con: a second clock; zone events and contacts at least 60 ms late at 20 Hz, against 1.4–1.8 ms per sample (motion-core simulation).

## Decision

Adopt C, extended by a wall rule for faces steeper than 30° (R3, R4), so that inclined obstacles are supported (owner, 2026-10-07). Lengths are scene units; the v1 designs fix 1 unit = 1 cm, as v0.3's `ENCODER_TO_CM` implies (`experiments/hallway04_experiment.py:84`; its comments say metres).

**R1. Time base and zones.** The motion core takes device samples from a first-in-first-out (FIFO) queue in seq order, each once, never in rAF; it alone integrates heading, turning each sub-step's displacement by the heading at its midpoint (yaw 0 looks down −Z, positive yaw is counter-clockwise from above, left is −X, as `experiments/hallway04_experiment.py:548-549`; rig calibration fixes sensor-axis signs). Integrals use device dt; the rig runtime sends displacements, not velocities. Without `world.step()`, the core calls `world.updateSceneQueries()` after colliders are created, enabled, disabled or replaced. After each sample, a sensors-only point query (`EXCLUDE_SOLIDS`, zone groups) on the agent's axis at feet + 1.15 (R3), matching v0.3's centre-based trial-end test (`:448`), finds the occupied zones; lint warns about zones thinner than one sample's displacement (7.05 units at 141 units/s and 20 samples/s).

**R2. Selection rule.** Use the hover KCC if and only if (a) the compiler caps every collider the agent can sweep at 40 units (R5) and (b) the pinned Rapier build passes the CI suite; upgrades failing (b) are not merged. Otherwise a superseding ADR adopts SlideMover, after fixing its seam trap (collapse near-parallel contact planes, or merge coplanar wall surfaces) and adding ledge and step logic.

**R3. Parameters**, validated together on walls, inclined faces, floors, falls, steps and ramps (2026-10-07), not yet with zones and gates. Another eye height, radius, skin or unit needs a suite re-run; the cap scales as about skin / 5e-4 (sweep time-of-impact error is 3–5e-3 at 40–80 units, ≈1e-1 at 1,000).

- Agent: upright cylinder, radius 0.5, from 0.3 above the feet to `eyeHeight` + 0.45 (4.15 at the default 3.7); no rigid body; moved by `setTranslation`. Every surface the sweep keeps outside the skin is then at least 0.47 from the eye, more than the near-plane corner distance (0.19 at 16:9, 0.43 at 48:9; vertical field of view 75°, near plane 0.1), so the camera cannot enter geometry at any yaw or pitch; nothing lower than `eyeHeight` + 0.47 can be passed under. R1's zone point query stays at feet + 1.15 on the axis.
- KCC: offset (skin) 0.02; `normalNudgeFactor` 0.01; sliding on; up +Y; autostep and snap-to-ground off; both slope angles 30°. Rapier 0.14.0 ignores the climb angle for horizontal pushes and 0.15.0–0.21.0 apply it to upward faces only, so R4's wall rule decides on every version; on an upgrade set both angles above 30° (45° and 89° tested on 0.21.0), since a limit of exactly 30° blocks 30° ramps there.
- Wall: a KCC collision or ground-ray hit whose normal n has n_y < cos 30° and a horizontal part |n_h| > 1e-3 (vertical walls, faces steeper than 30°, faces leaning over the agent, sides of balls and cones); its plane is h = n_h / |n_h|.
- All KCC calls, ground rays and guard queries: `EXCLUDE_SENSORS` and the agent's collision groups.
- Sub-steps: n = max(1, ⌈horizontal displacement / 0.25⌉). Gravity: scene value, default −981 units/s².
- Eye: feet + `eyeHeight` from the rig file, recorded per session; default 3.7, the height v0.3 sessions actually used (owner, 2026-10-07; adjustable per rig).

**R4. Step.** Each sub-step moves horizontally with at most six KCC calls. A call that reports no wall keeps its horizontal movement and ends the move. Otherwise the agent keeps the horizontal part of the first wall's `translationDeltaApplied` (where the KCC stopped at the skin) and drops the rest of that call; the remainder, the horizontal part of that collision's `translationDeltaRemaining`, loses its component into h and into the h of every earlier wall of the sub-step (dropped if it still points into one); one KCC call of 0.01·h steps off the face (cut at its own first wall); the next call moves the remainder; a remainder left after the sixth call is dropped. The sub-step then casts a ground ray from feet + 0.3 to feet − 0.3: a floor over 0.3 higher rejects the sub-step; a floor steeper than 30° is a wall facing the agent (h, or −h when the input runs along h), and the sub-step is redone once from the committed pose with that plane (the input loses its component into it, one call steps 0.01 off, the rule above moves the rest), rejected only if the ray again finds a floor too steep or too high; a floor in range sets the feet; none means airborne (`fall: "gravity"`) or a wall (`fall: "none"`). Once per sample, after the sub-steps, an airborne agent applies vy += g·dt and a vertical KCC sweep of vy·dt; if the sweep meets a wall, the agent keeps the sweep's 3-D movement up to the first walkable contact after that wall (it slides off the face); it lands only on a floor with n_y ≥ cos 30° while vy ≤ 0; horizontal input continues, as in both hover prototypes. `fall_start` (stamped with the sample whose ray found no floor) and `land` carry (seq, epoch, t_dev). Teleports, between samples, snap the feet to the floor, clear the airborne state and increment the epoch.

**R5. Partitioning.** The scene compiler (`compileScene`, schema v1) creates every scene collider; the motion core, the agent's.

1. Walkable horizontal surfaces: one collider per connected coplanar region, a cuboid if rectangular, else a triangle mesh (trimesh) without T-junctions (vertices on another triangle's edge), flagged `FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES | MERGE_DUPLICATE_VERTICES`; capped as in item 2 only if reachable from the side from a walkable region over 0.3 lower.
2. Sweepable geometry: no cuboid extent or triangle edge over 40 units (split cuboids into equal flush pieces, subdivide triangles; other primitives over 40 are lint errors); prefer solid cuboids, since a hollow trimesh turns a missed cast into an escape. No convex hull spans more than 20 units (split by vertical planes into flush convex pieces); cones are convex hulls (256-gon tested), never Rapier cone colliders (Rapier 0.14.0's cone misses intersections the guard relies on).
3. Sweepable faces may have any tilt: walkable ramps are climbed, steeper and overhanging faces are walls (R4). Walkable ramps are at most 28°: where a ramp meets the platform it leads to, the flat-bottomed cylinder meets a step of 0.5·tan α (0.289 at 30°), and 30° ramps could not be left at the top. Each side of a walkable ramp that is not flush with a wall or platform gets a rail: a cuboid 0.2 thick outside the side face, from the ramp's foot level to its top + `eyeHeight` + 0.1, along the whole ramp (the hover cannot step onto a ramp from its side; bare sides caused guard undos). Lint warns where a vertical face stands 0.52–1.04 beyond a ledge (an edge over a drop of more than 0.3): an agent walking off there hangs on the edge, feet 0.27 low (R4).
4. Zones are exempt.
5. A manifest records each collider's sources and the Rapier version; its hash enters every session.

**R6. Overlap guard.** After every horizontal sub-step and airborne sweep, the agent's cylinder shrunk to radius 0.48 is tested against solid colliders. A horizontal sub-step that hits is redone once from the committed pose with every collider the guard found treated as a wall: normal from `collider.contactShape` of the radius-0.5 cylinder at the committed pose (prediction 0.5), else from `collider.projectPoint` of the nearest of three axis points (feet + 0.3, middle, top), else the reverse of the sub-step direction; the input loses its components into those walls' h, one KCC call steps 0.01 along their summed h, and R4 moves the rest. A second hit, or a hit after an airborne sweep, undoes the call (restoring x, z, feet, airborne state and vy; heading still advances) and is logged with (seq, epoch, t_dev); recoveries are logged the same way. Expected undo count zero. Zones and the pose record use the final pose. The guard sees overlap, not crossings; the sweep prevents those (Validation 2).

**R7. One module.** One TypeScript module without browser dependencies holds the motion core and mover, for the motion worker on the rig and for Node (simulation, dry runs, CI, agent tools), with identical settings.

**R8. Pinning.** `package.json` pins `@dimforge/rapier3d-compat` exactly (0.14.0 today).

## Consequences

**Positive:** gain is 1 by construction; heading has one owner; eye height is floor + a declared constant; falls, steps, walkable ramps and inclined obstacles work; the camera cannot enter geometry; dry runs and replays reproduce the rig.

**Negative:** more colliders; sideways wobble ≤ 0.03 along capped vertical walls and ≤ 0.055 along inclined faces; 26–40% more time per sample than the hover without the wall rule (52 µs at 1,000 samples/s); steps of 0.284–0.3 and ramps over 28° are not walkable; nothing lower than `eyeHeight` + 0.47 can be passed under; walkable ramps are entered and left only at their ends; gated Rapier upgrades; the step-0 gain estimate (`src/utils/frameClock.js:27`) becomes v0.3-only; v0.3 data stay pilot data. The mover is 2.5-D (obstacles under 0.3 are stepped onto; step-ups lift the eye within one sample), and the cylinder departs from Rapier's recommended shapes, so the soak stays in CI.

**Risks:** Rapier's own slope limit cannot do R4's job: 0.14.0 ignores it for horizontal pushes (a standing capsule climbs 35–60° faces under a 30° limit) and 0.15.0–0.21.0 apply it to upward faces only, so the wall-rule cases gate upgrades. Rapier 0.14.0 misses some casts (starts within about 2e-5 of the skin, balls and cylinders hit nearly head-on, 31–33° faces with the 4.15-tall cylinder at about 2 ms per call); R6's recovery and R4's steep-floor slide absorb them, and penetrations inside the skin remain possible. An agent walking off a ledge toward a solid 0.53–1.04 beyond it hangs on the edge (R4, every hover variant); lint warns until R4 handles it.

**Documentation:** explicit slope, step and skin limits instead of forces are how game-engine character controllers work (Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot and Rapier itself, whose default walkable angles are about 45°); three-maze's values are stricter where the experiment needs it. Scene authors find every limit, its reason and what can be changed in [movement-and-collision-limits.md](../movement-and-collision-limits.md) (owner, 2026-10-08).

**Follow-up:** phase 0 (done 2026-10-08): `test/` and `*.md` no longer ignored, Vitest and TypeScript type checking on Windows CI (R7; owner, 2026-10-07), exact pin. Phase 1: motion-core module; compiler partitioning, lint and manifest; maze migrator (`public/mazes`: six walls over 40 units become 26 pieces; each hallway's three floor boxes, one); protocol v1 device samples, removing the backend's velocity, heading and stale-pose code; firmware v2 sequence numbers and microsecond timestamps; Rapier upgraded to the then-latest version, validated by this record's Validation suite, then pinned again ([ADR-0004](0004-dependency-versions.md)).

## Validation

A CI gate (Node, pinned Rapier, Windows) runs the shipped configuration on every change to the motion module, compiler or Rapier. Overlap: deeper than the skin. Retention: realised ÷ commanded displacement along a wall.

1. **Long walls:** 200, 400 and 600 units at 1–75°; 30 and 141 units/s at 120 samples/s, 100 units/s at 20. Pass: no overlap; retention ≥ 99%; sideways range reported.
2. **Thin walls:** 0.1 units thick, 141–1,000 units/s, up to 50 units per sample, 5–90°. Pass: no crossing.
3. **Seams, corners, wedges:** coplanar seams at 60–89°, a T-junction, a 90° corner, 30–90° wedges, the side of a 200-unit raised platform from below. Pass: contained; retention ≥ 99%.
4. **Soak:** the adjudication's closed 400 × 120 room (402-unit walls, 18 obstacles); 4 seeds × 5 min of random running at 5–141 units/s; 20, 120 and 250 samples/s; cuboid and trimesh walls. Pass: no overlap, escape or guard revert.
5. **Floors, drops, steps, ramps, inclined faces:** feet range ≤ 1e-4 over floor T-junctions; a 20-unit drop lands within 2 samples of free fall at 20, 120 and 250 samples/s; a 0.28 step climbed and ≥ 0.29 blocked, also under an overhang; 28° ramps climbed up onto the platform they lead to and descended, head-on and 30° off-axis, with their railed sides blocked and no guard undo; 31–75° faces blocked. Flat faces of 31–150°, balls of radius 3, 10, 25 and 60, hull cones (base radius 10, height 20) upright and inverted, a lying cylinder, a tumbled cube and a split slab, approached at 5–90° at 30 and 141 units/s and 20, 120 and 250 samples/s, and two inclined soak rooms (4 seeds × 5 min per rate; one with a platform, railed ramps and objects near its edge): no overlap with the true shapes, no guard undo, retention ≥ 99% along flat faces, sideways range ≤ 0.08, eye-point clearance ≥ 0.43 and near-plane clearance > 0 at 16:9 to 32:9. Falls onto steep faces and toward objects land on the floor below; an adversarial cast-miss census gives no guard undo.
6. **Zones and gates:** zones never block; enter and exit fire in the sample that carries the centre across the boundary; lint flags zones thinner than one sample's displacement; a gate enabled at run time, with no `world.step()`, stops the agent at its analytic position.
7. **Arrival independence:** one sample log, delivered per sample and in per-frame batches under 8 frame schedules (56.5, 60, 75, 120, 144 Hz; 2% long frames; a 250 ms stall; a 2 s hidden window): gain 1.0000 on contact-free segments; bit-identical per-seq poses and (seq, epoch) events. Pinned Node and Electron builds replay one log bit-identically (pass/fail); differences are removed before v1.0-rc, for example by a deterministic sin/cos.
8. **Compiler:** examples and migrated mazes respect R5; a 41-unit wall, a convex hull over 20 units across, a Rapier cone collider, a walkable ramp steeper than 28° and an unrailed ramp side fail lint (a 45° face no longer does); a vertical face 0.52–1.04 beyond a ledge warns.

**On the rig (phase 3):** known trajectories (firmware v2 script mode, or a motorised ball) at 20, 250 and 1,000 Hz on 60, 120 and 144 Hz displays, with stalls and a 5 s hidden window: |g − 1| < 1e-3, identical per-seq pose records, no guard revert.

## When to revisit

- A needed Rapier upgrade fails the suite, or Rapier's KCC changes.
- A required scene breaks R5 (for example imported meshes, which are outside v1.0).
- Any overlap or guard revert; |g − 1| ≥ 1e-3; sideways range above 0.1 units on capped walls.
- A fixed SlideMover, with falls and steps added, passes Validation 1–8 without wobble or seam trap at no more than the hover's cost.
